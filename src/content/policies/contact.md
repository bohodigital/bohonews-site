# Contact Boho News

Boho News uses two public email addresses.

## General contact

[contact@bohonews.com](mailto:contact@bohonews.com)

Use this address for:

- general questions;
- news tips;
- public documents and datasets;
- source inquiries;
- support questions;
- privacy requests;
- accessibility feedback;
- copyright complaints;
- legal correspondence;
- professional and partnership inquiries.

Helpful subject lines include:

- `Tip`
- `Public Record`
- `Support`
- `Privacy`
- `Accessibility`
- `Copyright`
- `Legal Notice`

## Corrections

[corrections@bohonews.com](mailto:corrections@bohonews.com)

Use this address for:

- factual corrections;
- disputed quotations;
- data or chart errors;
- misleading headlines or captions;
- missing material context;
- requests for clarification;
- responses to serious allegations.

Include the article URL, the exact disputed material, the evidence supporting your request, and a way to contact you.

## Security and confidentiality warning

Ordinary email is not a secure tip line.

Sending a message does not automatically create a confidential-source, attorney-client, employment, or contractual relationship.

Do not send highly sensitive information through ordinary email when disclosure could endanger you or someone else.

We will publish a secure tip channel only after it is properly configured and tested.

## Attachments and rights

Do not send material you do not have the right to disclose.

When sending photographs, recordings, documents, or datasets, explain:

- where the material came from;
- when it was created or obtained;
- whether it has been edited;
- who owns it;
- whether publication could create safety or privacy risks.

## Response time

We read serious messages, but we cannot promise a response to every submission.

Urgency, evidence, public importance, safety, and newsroom capacity affect response time.

Repeated harassment, spam, malware, threats, impersonation, and abusive automated messages may be blocked or reported.
