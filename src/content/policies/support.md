# Support Boho News

## Support reporting, not access

Boho News is building an independent public-interest newsroom capable of publishing quickly and investigating deeply.

That requires money.

Records cost money. Travel costs money. Photography, data, hosting, insurance, security, legal review, subscriptions, equipment, and skilled labor cost money. Pretending journalism can be produced indefinitely through unpaid sacrifice is not a funding model. It is a method for ensuring that only wealthy institutions can sustain reporting.

Financial support helps us build the capacity to do harder work.

It does not buy influence.

## What support funds

Support may be used for:

- fair compensation for reporting, editing, engineering, research, photography, design, administration, and operations;
- public-record requests and litigation;
- reporting travel;
- data and document acquisition;
- source subscriptions and reference services;
- photography, audio, video, maps, and graphics;
- legal, security, and safety support;
- accessibility work;
- hosting, storage, and publishing infrastructure;
- insurance and professional services;
- long-term reserves and institutional stability.

## What support does not buy

A contribution does not purchase:

- favorable coverage;
- negative coverage of someone else;
- access to unpublished reporting;
- the identity of a confidential source;
- advance control over a story;
- protection from scrutiny;
- a correction unsupported by evidence;
- editorial, hiring, or personnel authority.

We may decline or return support that creates a serious conflict, concealed influence, legal risk, or mission conflict.

## Current financial-support status

Boho News is operated on a nonprofit basis by Republic of Bohemia LLC while a dedicated nonprofit structure is developed.

Boho News does not currently claim IRS recognition under Section 501(c)(3). Financial support is not tax-deductible unless we expressly confirm otherwise in writing.

A financial-contribution tool is not currently active on this page. When an approved payment channel is enabled, this page will identify the processor, recurring-payment terms, cancellation process, refund rules, privacy implications, and current tax status before payment information is collected.

## Other ways to support the newsroom

You can help without sending money.

- Read and share work that matters.
- Link to our reporting and primary-document pages.
- Send corrections when we get something wrong.
- Point us toward public records, audits, filings, hearings, datasets, and local consequences.
- Offer technical, legal, scientific, financial, accessibility, language, or subject-matter expertise.
- Tell us which institutions and issues receive too little scrutiny.
- Submit original photographs or documents when you have the right to share them.

## Tips and documents

General tips and documents may be sent to [contact@bohonews.com](mailto:contact@bohonews.com).

Ordinary email is not a secure channel and does not automatically create confidentiality. Do not send highly sensitive source material through ordinary email when disclosure could endanger you or someone else.

We will publish a secure tip channel only after it is properly configured and tested.

## Donor privacy

We do not sell donor information.

We do not publicly identify a supporter without permission unless disclosure is legally required or necessary to explain a material conflict involving our coverage.

Payment processors may collect information under their own policies when financial support becomes available. We will update this page and our Privacy Policy before enabling payment collection.

## Questions

Questions about support may be sent to [contact@bohonews.com](mailto:contact@bohonews.com) with the subject line `Support`.
